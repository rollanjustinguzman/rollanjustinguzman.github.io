Zen Marine Charter, Destin, Florida

Selling the last weekends of the season.

A luxury yacht charter running a September discount against a hard deadline. The brief: make a price promotion feel like an invitation, not a clearance sale. So the offer sits quietly under the image instead of shouting over it.

Why it works

Charter buyers decide fast and compare on feel, not features. The set leads with water and light, keeps the discount to one line, and puts every practical question (how many people, how long, what's included, what it costs) on a single card that can go straight into a DM.

Systems

What sits behind the content.

Content only compounds if a system is holding it. On the accounts I run, that means one board where every piece has an owner, a keyword target, and a state.

The same applies after the click. In GoHighLevel I set up the pipeline stages, the email and SMS sequences that fire against them, and the Appointwise agent that qualifies a lead and books it. In Dubsado and HoneyBook, the equivalent for contracts, invoices, and onboarding.
